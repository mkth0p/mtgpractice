How this list was made

  • Underplayed: every commander has fewer than 5,000 decks on its EDHREC page (read on 2026-11-02). About 60 were checked. Popular ones like Codie, Sisay, Obeka, Hashaton, Alania, Kilo and Kefka were dropped for being over that line.
  • Very high Bracket 4: the commander has to be the engine or a combo piece. Colors-only commanders, copied cEDH shells and hard stax plans were left out; where a card leans that way, its card says so.
  • Combos: taken from Commander Spellbook searches (deck counts are theirs), and each one was checked by hand to fit the commander's colors. Lines that needed off-color cards were dropped.
  • Card text: from the Card-Forge card database.
  • Power dots (out of 5) are our own read of each commander's Bracket 4 ceiling. Nothing was simulated, and prices were not checked yet.
  • No Marvel cards.
