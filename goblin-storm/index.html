GoblinStorm

The Secret Lair Commander Deck: Goblin Storm, with new art by Wizard of Barge. Zada, Hedron Grinder copies every spell you aim at her once for each other creature you control, so a one-mana cantrip with ten Goblins out draws eleven cards. Make Goblins, cast cheap spells on Zada, then finish with storm or a huge attack. Here's the full manual: a guide in steps, every card with rulings, the combos, prices, a checklist to play with, a quiz, printable proxies and a real game to practise against.

How the deck wins

Goblins, Zada, copies, storm

  1. 1
    Go wide

    Krenko, Mob Boss, Dragon Fodder, Empty the Warrens and Siege-Gang Commander fill the board with Goblin tokens.

  2. 2
    Point it at Zada

    Cast Expedite, Crimson Wisps or Ancestors' Aid on Zada, Hedron Grinder. Every other creature gets a copy: cards, haste, Treasures.

  3. 3
    Turn it into mana

    Storm-Kiln Artist makes a Treasure for every cast and copy. Brightstone Ritual and Battle Hymn pay for the board.

  4. 4
    Finish

    A big Grapeshot, a twenty-Goblin Empty the Warrens with Impact Tremors, or a Haze of Rage loop and one attack. See the combos.

    Combos & kills

    How to actually win

    The deck has one two-card loop and a lot of big turns. Build a board, cast the cheap spells on Zada, Hedron Grinder for cards, mana and haste, then finish with storm or a drain. Tell your table about the Haze of Rage loop before you play.

    Combo 1Unbounded power and stormNo. 01 · Your main phaseStorm-Kiln Artist + Haze of Rage
    +
    You need
    Artist on the battlefield, Haze in hand, and three spells already cast this turn (two with Ruby Medallion).
    Speed
    Sorcery. Do it before combat with creatures that can attack.
    1. Cast Haze of Rage with buyback for {3}{R}. Storm copies it once for each spell cast before it.
    2. Storm-Kiln Artist makes a Treasure for the cast and for each copy: 1 + storm. From storm 3 that's 4 Treasures, enough for the next Haze.
    3. Haze goes back to your hand. Cast it again: storm is one higher each time, so every loop gives more power and more spare Treasures.
    4. Stop when the team is big enough, then attack. Spare Treasures can pay for a Grapeshot at the end.

    Copies count for Artist, not for storm

    Artist triggers when you cast or copy a spell, so every storm copy makes a Treasure. Storm counts only spells cast, so the copies don't raise the storm count.

    Count it out

    Storm turns

    Each copy of Grapeshot can hit a different target, each Empty the Warrens copy makes two Goblins, and each Haze of Rage copy pumps the whole team.

    Combo 2Damage as Goblins enterNo. 02 · Any token turnImpact Tremors + General Kreat + a token storm
    ++
    1. Each creature entering under your control is 1 damage to each opponent from Impact Tremors, and 1 more from General Kreat, the Boltbringer.
    2. A sixth-spell Empty the Warrens makes 12 Goblins: 24 damage to every opponent with both out.
    3. Roaming Throne naming Goblin makes Kreat trigger twice. It doesn't copy Impact Tremors, which is an enchantment.
    Combo 3Sacrifice drainNo. 03 · Instant speedGoblin Bombardment + Pashalik Mons
    ++
    1. Sacrifice a Goblin to Goblin Bombardment: 1 damage to any target.
    2. Pashalik Mons deals 1 more when a Goblin you control dies, and Boggart Shenanigans 1 more. That's 3 per Goblin.
    3. Do it in response to a board wipe, or at the end of a turn to finish a player.

    Strength

    A strong Bracket 3 deck

    Bracket 3 is "Upgraded": up to three Game Changers, no mass land destruction, and two-card combos only late in the game. The deck has no Game Changers. The bracket checker on scrollvault.net rates it Bracket 3, 6.2 out of 10, because Storm-Kiln Artist and Haze of Rage form a two-card loop. That loop needs three spells first, so it is a late-game combo. Tell your table about it.

    1casualupgradedoptimizedcEDH 10

    Why it's strong

    • Zada turns every cheap spell into a whole-board spell: one Expedite can draw ten cards.
    • 15 Goblin makers and 13 rituals and mana cards: big turns come out of nowhere.
    • Many ways to finish: storm, drains as Goblins enter or die, and huge attacks.
    • Mono-red, 36 lands: the mana is easy.

    What holds it back

    • Board wipes hit hard: the deck is mostly 1/1 tokens. Sacrifice them in response.
    • Zada dies to every removal spell, and Swiftfoot Boots is the only protection.
    • Only one real tutor (Goblin Matron), so the deck can draw the wrong half.
    • Light interaction: Chaos Warp, Vandalblast, Blasphemous Act and a few damage spells.
