## Key rules findings (from Etrata's official rulings, via API)
- **Instants and sorceries:** "If something tries to turn a face-down instant or sorcery card on the battlefield face up, reveal that card to show all players it's an instant or sorcery card. The permanent remains on the battlefield face down." This is the "if you can't" case, so Etrata exiles the card and you cast it free.
  - In practice, the free-cast branch only applies to **instants and sorceries**, or when something like Karlov Watchdog stops turning face up.
  - A face-down artifact, enchantment, land or planeswalker card is turned face up as that permanent. It is not cast.
  - So a manifested **Omniscience** turns face up and stays on the battlefield, which still works, but it is not "cast."
  - This is my reading of the rulings: only instants and sorceries are named as unable to turn face up.
- **Free casts:** "If the spell you cast has {X} in its mana cost, you must choose 0 as the value of X." Also: "you can't choose to cast it for any alternative costs. You can, however, pay additional costs, such as kicker costs."
- **Timing:** Etrata's ability is an activated ability you can use at instant speed. A spell cast during its resolution ignores sorcery timing, so you can cast Time Stretch on an opponent's end step.
- **No ETB triggers:** "Because the permanent is on the battlefield both before and after it's turned face up, turning a permanent face up doesn't cause any enters-the-battlefield abilities to trigger." "When you cast" triggers also never fire, for example on Kozilek or Ulamog.
- **Morph and disguise cards:** a face-down creature cast with morph or disguise loses its way to turn face up if it loses abilities. Manifested and cloaked creature cards don't have that problem.
- **Megamorph:** the +1/+1 counter is only added when you pay the megamorph cost ("Turn it face up any time for its megamorph cost and put a +1/+1 counter on it"). Etrata's {2}{U}{B} flip gives no counter.
- **Face-down creatures have no creature types.** Silumgar Assassin and Ruthless Ripper are only Assassins for Etrata's cloak trigger while face up.
- **Cloak vs. manifest:** cloaked cards are 2/2 with **ward {2}**. Manifested cards have no ward.

### Ixidron and Etrata
- Oracle (API): "As Ixidron enters the battlefield, turn all other nontoken creatures face down. (They're 2/2 creatures.) Ixidron's power and toughness are each equal to the number of face-down creatures on the battlefield."
- Rulings (API): "Creatures turned face down by Ixidron are 2/2 creatures with no text, no name, no subtypes, no expansion symbol, and no mana cost." Also: "If Ixidron and another creature are entering the battlefield at the same time, the other creature enters the battlefield face up."
- The Ixidron rulings don't explicitly cover turning those creatures face up. The Illusionary Mask ruling states the general principle: "You may not turn a face-down permanent face up unless it would have morph while face up or an effect specifically allows you to turn it face up."
- **Gotcha:** if Etrata is on the battlefield, Ixidron turns **Etrata herself** face down. She loses her ability, so nothing grants the flip anymore, and she is stuck as a vanilla 2/2. Cast Ixidron **before** Etrata.
- Once Etrata is out, your face-down creatures can flip for 4 each (with no ETB). Opponents' creatures stay as vanilla 2/2s forever unless they have morph, because Etrata only grants the flip to creatures you control.
- Ixidron doesn't affect tokens, so it's poor against token swarms. It's good against precons built around big creatures. CK price: $0.69, https://www.cardkingdom.com/mtg/commander-2019/ixidron

## 2025–2026 sets
- **Lorwyn Eclipsed:** its release notes (https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/lorwyn-eclipsed-release-notes) list no face-down, morph, disguise, manifest or cloak cards.
  - It brings back **Changeling**, and changelings count as Assassins for Etrata's trigger. I didn't verify specific U/B changelings.
- **Tarkir Dragonstorm:** the Commander decklist page shows no face-down mechanics. Any morph reprints there were not verified.
- **Edge of Eternities, Final Fantasy, Spider-Man, Avatar:** I found no face-down cards for these. That's an absence in my search results, not proof. Final Fantasy Commander did reprint Rise of the Dark Realms.
- **Foundations (late 2024):** Sire of Seven Deaths, covered above.
- **Duskmourn (2024):** Abhorrent Oculus and Cursed Windbreaker, covered above. Other manifest dread cards I found are white, green, red or multicolor outside Dimir. I didn't fully list black ones from DSK.

## Ranked shortlist (best 8 for this deck)
1. **Silumgar Assassin** ($0.36): a turn-3 face-down blocker that kills a creature at instant speed for {2}{B}, then becomes an evasive Assassin that turns on Etrata's cloak trigger.
2. **Cryptic Coat** ($0.49): adds an unblockable warded body on turn 3, and you can bounce and recast it to keep adding face-down creatures for Etrata to flip.
3. **Stratus Dancer** ($0.40): a 3-drop that holds up a {1}{U} counterspell, so you don't tap out into punishment.
4. **Ugin's Mastery** ($0.69): every morph or disguise you cast also manifests a card, which doubles the board. Attacking with 6+ power gives a free flip.
5. **Void Winnower** ($20.49): the best "flip for 4" target. Static abilities only; it stops even-MV spells and all token blockers.
6. **Kadena's Silencer** ($0.40): a 2-mana face-up cost that counters all opponents' abilities on the stack, a blowout against precon ETB and attack triggers.
7. **Panoptic Projektor** ($0.59): face-down spells cost {0}, and every "turned face up" trigger fires twice.
8. **Willbender** ($0.25): a 2-mana redirect that protects Etrata or turns opponents' removal against them.

Next in line: Ruthless Ripper (a free-flip deathtouch Assassin), Thieving Amalgam, Rise of the Dark Realms, Sire of Seven Deaths, Exit Specialist and Ransom Note.
