## Brackets

The official format page still labels the brackets "beta" (fetched 2026-10-07). The definitions below are from October 21, 2025, and the February 2026 update left them unchanged.

**Bracket 4, Optimized:** decks that don't follow the cEDH metagame (that's Bracket 5), built to be lethal, consistent and fast. Game Changers there are expected to be fast mana, snowballing engines, free disruption and tutors. Win conditions are efficient and instant; gameplay is explosive, with efficient disruption to match. Players should expect at least four turns before anyone wins or loses.

**Bracket 5, cEDH:** decks built for the cEDH metagame, often from existing cEDH knowledge, tools or decklists. Games can end on any turn.

Implications for this project:

- A deck that usually wins on turn 4 sits at the fast edge of Bracket 4; turns 5–6 are the expected window (DECISIONS.md, D3).
- Copying published cEDH lists wholesale is, by the definition, a Bracket 5 signal. Mine them for cards, but build for the Bracket 4 field.
