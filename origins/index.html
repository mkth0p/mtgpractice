How much to trust it

For a tournament: the best three decks

The Crimson Cup format is Conquest with one ban: bring three decks with different Legendaries, each player bans one, then you must win once with each of your two decks left. Bans and deck picks were solved as small games on the simulated matchups, for every trio of Legendaries.

Card ratings

For every card, in every list that plays it: how the deck did in the games where the card was drawn, against how that same deck did on average. A plus means the deck wins more when it sees the card. Cards are drawn at random, so this is close to a fair test, but it rates a card inside the lists that play it, not in a vacuum.
