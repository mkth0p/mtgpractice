Selesnya creature combo · Bracket 4

CorruptedMiku

The other side of the Miku Secret Lair: Shalai, Voice of Plenty, printed as Miku, Voice Over All, at the head of a fast green-white combo deck. Shalai gives you and your other creatures hexproof, cheap creatures stop opponents from casting spells on your turn, and two cards win the game. Here's the full manual: a guide in steps, every card with rulings, the combos, a checklist to play with, a quiz to learn it fast, and a real game to practise against.

How the deck wins

Ramp, shield, silence, combo

  1. 1
    Ramp fast

    One-mana dorks, Sol Ring, the moxen and Gaea's Cradle get you to four mana on turn 2.

  2. 2
    Put up the shield

    Shalai, Voice of Plenty gives you and your other creatures hexproof. Swords, Path and targeted discard stop working on you.

  3. 3
    Silence the table

    Grand Abolisher, Kutzil, Malamet Exemplar, Voice of Victory and Silence stop opponents casting spells on your turn.

  4. 4
    Two cards win

    Archangel of Thune + Spike Feeder, Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Walking Ballista, or Devoted Druid + Vizier of Remedies. See the combos.

    Combos & kills

    How to actually win

    Every win is a two-card combo or a Craterhoof turn. Assemble the pieces behind Shalai, Voice of Plenty, go off on a turn when opponents can't cast spells, and keep a tutor for the missing piece. Tell your table about the combos before you play.

    Combo 1Infinite life and countersNo. 01 · Instant, no manaArchangel of Thune + Spike Feeder
    +
    You need
    Both on the battlefield and a +1/+1 counter on Spike Feeder. No mana.
    Speed
    Instant. Feeder's ability doesn't tap, so a summoning-sick Feeder works too.
    1. Remove a +1/+1 counter from Spike Feeder: gain 2 life.
    2. Archangel of Thune triggers: a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control, Feeder included.
    3. Repeat as often as you like. Infinite life, and every creature you control as big as you want.
    4. Win by attacking, or add Walking Ballista: its counters grow too, then ping.
    Combo 2Infinite damageNo. 02 · InstantHeliod + Walking Ballista
    +
    You need
    Ballista with 2 or more counters, and {1}{W} to give it lifelink with Heliod.
    Speed
    Instant.
    1. Pay {1}{W}: Walking Ballista gains lifelink until end of turn.
    2. Remove a counter: Ballista deals 1 damage. You gain 1 life.
    3. Heliod, Sun-Crowned triggers: put a +1/+1 counter back on Ballista. Repeat until the table is dead.

    Two counters, not one

    With one counter, Ballista dies as you pay the cost, and Heliod's counter has nowhere to go. Spike Feeder with Heliod is infinite life too.

    Combo 3Infinite green manaNo. 03 · Your turnDevoted Druid + Vizier of Remedies
    +
    1. Tap Devoted Druid for {G}. It needs to have been yours since your turn began, or haste from Lightning Greaves.
    2. Put a -1/-1 counter on it to untap it. Vizier of Remedies puts one fewer: zero.
    3. Repeat: unlimited {G}. Spend it on Walking Ballista ({4} per counter), Shalai's {4}{G}{G} or Finale of Devastation for X 10 or more.

    Plan B

    Natural Order into Craterhoof

    At 40 life a player, Craterhoof needs a wide board. Six or seven attackers kill one opponent; the whole table needs about ten or eleven.

    Strength

    A Bracket 4 combo deck

    Bracket 4 is "Optimized": no limit on Game Changers, two-card combos or fast mana, but not built for the cEDH tournament meta. This deck plays 15 of the 53 Game Changers on the official list (checked against the 21 Oct 2025 list and the 9 Feb 2026 additions), and none of its cards is banned. It is fast and consistent, and it wins with two cards. Against Bracket 3 decks it is unfair; against real cEDH decks it is a step slower.

    1casualupgradedoptimizedcEDH 10

    Why it's strong

    • Four two-card combos and 16 tutors to find them.
    • Shalai's hexproof stops targeted removal on the combo pieces.
    • Grand Abolisher, Kutzil, Voice of Victory, Silence and Orim's Chant keep opponents from answering on your turn.
    • 21 one-mana cards and a lot of fast mana: turn-2 and turn-3 wins happen.

    What holds it back

    • Board wipes ignore hexproof. Keep Teferi's Protection or Flawless Maneuver for them.
    • Shalai doesn't protect herself, and Lightning Greaves' shroud also stops your own Heliod and Giver from targeting.
    • Deafening Silence limits you too: one noncreature spell a turn, and the deck has 36.
    • Grafdigger's Cage, Torpor Orb, Null Rod and Cursed Totem each stop a combo or the tutors.

    Game Changers in the deck (15)

    Gaea's Cradle, Ancient Tomb, Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Chrome Mox, Mox Diamond, The One Ring, Smothering Tithe, Worldly Tutor, Enlightened Tutor, Crop Rotation, Natural Order, Survival of the Fittest, Drannith Magistrate, Teferi's Protection. Vorinclex, Voice of Hunger was removed from the list in October 2025. The brackets are still in beta, so check the official list before a game.

    Your other decks

    Corrupted Miku vs Miku

    Same Secret Lair, two very different decks. The Trostani Miku deck is a Bracket 3 lifegain and tokens deck that wins with big attacks. Corrupted Miku is a Bracket 4 combo deck that wins with two cards, often before the other decks are set up. Don't bring it to a Bracket 3 table. This comes from reading both lists, not from playtesting.
